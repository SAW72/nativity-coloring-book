# The Nativity Coloring Book – Complete Scripture Edition

A high-quality, printable black-and-white coloring book covering the **entire Nativity story** from the Annunciation to the visit of the Wise Men.

**Based faithfully on the Holy Scriptures** (Luke 1–2 & Matthew 1–2) with:
- Full spelled-out key Bible verses on every page
- Short story summaries
- Original Greek and Hebrew language notes and insights

## Pages (in order)

1. **01_Title_Cover.jpg** – Title / Cover page
2. **02_Annunciation.jpg** – The Angel Gabriel Visits Mary (Luke 1:26-38)
3. **03_Joseph_Angel.jpg** – An Angel Appears to Joseph (Matthew 1:18-25)
4. **04_Journey_Bethlehem.jpg** – The Journey to Bethlehem (Luke 2:1-5)
5. **05_Birth_Jesus.jpg** – The Birth of Jesus (Luke 2:6-7)
6. **06_Angels_Shepherds.jpg** – The Angels and the Shepherds (Luke 2:8-14)
7. **07_Shepherds_Visit.jpg** – The Shepherds Visit Jesus (Luke 2:15-20)
8. **08_Wise_Men.jpg** – The Wise Men Visit Jesus (Matthew 2:1-12)
9. **09_Closing.jpg** – Closing / End of the Nativity Story

## How to Print & Assemble

1. Download all 9 JPG pages (or the complete PDF if available).
2. Print on letter or A4 paper (cardstock recommended for durability).
3. For a booklet: print double-sided if possible, fold or staple along the left edge.
4. Color with pencils, markers, or crayons!
5. Share the Good News!
