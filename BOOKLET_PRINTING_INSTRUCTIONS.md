# Booklet Printing Instructions

## Nativity Coloring Book – Booklet Version

**File:** [`Nativity_Coloring_Book_BOOKLET.pdf`](Nativity_Coloring_Book_BOOKLET.pdf) (repository root)

This version is specially imposed for **saddle-stitch / folded booklet** printing.

### Specs
- 3 sheets (6 sides) of landscape letter paper (11" x 8.5")
- Each sheet has two half-pages side-by-side
- When printed double-sided, nested, folded in half, and stapled in the center, you get a 12-page half-letter (5.5" x 8.5") booklet
- Original 9 story pages + 3 blank pages (for notes or extra coloring)

### How to Print
1. Download [`Nativity_Coloring_Book_BOOKLET.pdf`](Nativity_Coloring_Book_BOOKLET.pdf)
2. Print **double-sided** (flip on short edge / landscape)
3. Make sure the printer does not re-order or scale the pages
4. Nest the 3 sheets in order (outer sheet first)
5. Fold in half along the center line
6. Staple 2–3 times along the fold (saddle stitch)
7. Enjoy your pocket-sized Nativity coloring book!

### Page order after folding
1. Title Cover  
2. Annunciation  
3. Joseph’s Dream  
4. Journey to Bethlehem  
5. Birth of Jesus  
6. Angels & Shepherds  
7. Shepherds Visit  
8. Wise Men  
9. Closing  
10–12. Blank pages
